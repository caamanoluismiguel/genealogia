## RAMA TRONCAL — Lords of the House of Noya (Main Trunk)

### Gen 1: Rodrigo García de Caamaño (~1100s)

- Participated in conquest of Cuenca and Baeza (1146)
- Married **Ilduara Fernández**
- Son: Sancho García

### Gen 2: Sancho García de Caamaño (d. 1155)

- Died at siege of Andújar
- Married (1st) **Constanza Fernández de Temes**, (2nd) **Urraca García Gorred**
- Son: Fernán García

### Gen 3: Fernán García de Caamaño

- Succeeded at Noya
- Married **Aldonza de Moscoso** (Altamira family)
- Son: Garci Fernández

### Gen 4: Garci Fernández de Caamaño

- Served King Fernando III in conquests of Córdoba and Seville
- Married **Sancha de Saavedra**
- Son: Fernán García

### Gen 5: Fernán García de Caamaño (II)

- Married **Constanza Suárez de Figueroa**
- Sons: Garci Fernández, **Vasco** (founded Portuguese Camões line — yes, related to the poet Luís de Camões)

### Gen 6: Garci Fernández de Caamaño (II)

- Followed D. Enrique de Trastámara
- Married **María Pardo de Junqueras**
- Sons: Rui Fernández, Frey Juan (Comendador of Caracuel)

### Gen 7: Rui Fernández de Caamaño

- → Continues Main Trunk (Casa de Noya line)
- → ALSO father of García de Caamaño "El Hermoso" (Second Branch — Villagarcía)

## SEGUNDA RAMA — Lords of Rubianes, Marquises of Villagarcía

### Gen 7 (branch): García Rodríguez de Caamaño

- Received O Salnés lands from Archbishop of Santiago (1390)
- Reclaimed Tuy from Portuguese (1379)
- Married **María Pérez das Mariñas**
- Son: Rui Fernández

### Gen 8: Rui Fernández de Caamaño (d. 1462)

- Lord of Caamaño house, alcaide of Barreira fortress
- Married **Inés Fernández de Silva y Fajardo**
- Son: García "El Hermoso"

### Gen 9: García de Caamaño "El Hermoso"

- Lord of Rubianes, Vista Alegre, Arealonga, Campolongo, Chapín, Coiro
- **FOUNDED VILLAGARCÍA DE AROUSA** (charter May 2, 1441)
- Built Pazo de Rubianes (inscription dated 1411)
- Married **Inés de Mendoza y Sotomayor** (daughter of Mariscal Suero Gómez de Sotomayor)
- Son: García "El Alto"

### Gen 10: García de Caamaño de Mendoza "El Alto" (d. Jan 1540)

- Served Catholic Monarchs in Granada campaign
- Served Emperor Charles V
- Granted mayorazgo authority (1524)
- Named Señor de la Casa de Rubianes by Charles I (Feb 4, 1535)
- Settled dispute with San Martín Pinario monastery (1508)
- Married **Constanza Sánchez de Bendaña** (daughter of Vasco Guillelmes, councilman of Noya)
- Buried in Franciscan convent chapel at Noya
- Children:
  - **García Rodríguez** (heir — continues Rubianes line)
  - **Álvaro de Sotomayor** (received Vista Alegre and Barrantes — Third Branch)
  - Rodrigo de Mendoza (abbot of Teverga)
  - - 4 others

### Gen 11: García Rodríguez de Caamaño y Mendaña

- Lord of Rubianes and Villagarcía jurisdiction
- Married (1534) **Margarita de Montoto y Figueroa** (daughter of La Coruña Regidor Fernando de Montoto)
- Son: García de Caamaño y Mendoza

### Gen 12: García de Caamaño y Mendoza (IV)

- Lord of Rubianes and Cornazo
- Lost Villagarcía jurisdiction (dispute with Santiago Archbishop)
- Married **Juana de Figueroa y Villamarín** (daughter of Juan Núñez Pardo de Cela)
- Son: José Caamaño Figueroa
- Daughter: Constanza (married Juan de Gayoso y Lugo)

### Gen 13: José Caamaño Figueroa

- Served in Burgundy wars
- Married (1st) ?, (2nd) ?, (3rd) **Ana María Caamaño de Mendoza** (cousin)
- Daughter: Antonia

### Gen 14: Antonia de Caamaño y Mendoza

- Inherited Rubianes estates
- Married **Mauro Caamaño de Mendoza y Sotomayor** (uncle, from Third Branch)
- **First Marquise of Villagarcía and Viscountess of Barrantes**

### Gen 15: Antonio Domingo Caamaño Mendoza

- **Second Marquis of Villagarcía, Viscount of Barrantes**
- Married **Juana Ibáñez de Rivera**
- Son: Antonio José (Third Marquis)

### How to Bridge the Gap

The key fact: **José Tomás was born in the PARISH of Caamaño (Porto do Son)** — the very place the surname originates. This means your line likely descends from the common folk of that parish (NOT necessarily from the noble Rubianes/Villagarcía branch, which was based in Pontevedra).

**Research needed:**

1. **FamilySearch: Porto do Son parish records (1648-1951)** — search for José Tomás Caamaño's baptism
2. **Approximate José Tomás's birth date** — if Luis Miguel is ~35 (born ~1991) and there are 5 generations between them, estimate ~25 years/gen → José Tomás born ~1850-1870
3. **Porto do Son civil registry** — births, marriages after 1870 are well-documented
4. **Emigration records (PARES Movimientos Migratorios)** — José Tomás's departure from A Coruña to Colombia
5. Once José Tomás's parents are found → trace upward through Porto do Son parish records

**The parish of Santa María de Caamaño has 620 inhabitants today.** In the 1800s it was smaller. Finding José Tomás's baptism record should be achievable — limited number of Caamaños in a small parish.
