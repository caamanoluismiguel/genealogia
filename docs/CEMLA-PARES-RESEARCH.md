### Place Name: Santa María de Caamaño

**PARES record:** "Expediente de comprobación de bienes, rentas y cargas de la feligresía de Santa María de Caamaño (Noya, Santiago)" — AGS, 1761-1764, DGR,1RE,1166,6

This is the **parish of Caamaño** near Noia — the toponymic origin of the surname. If this is from the Catastro de Ensenada, it lists every household head with property and family members.

### Moctezuma Archive Connection

Juan José Caamaño's filiation proof (1797) is in the Archivo de la Nobleza, Sección Moctezuma. This does NOT mean descent from Moctezuma — the Moctezuma ducal archive collected papers from many Galician noble families through marriage and legal connections.

## 3. Genealogías de Colombia — Documented Colombian Lines

Source: _Genealogías de Colombia_ by Fidel Botero Arango (www.genealogiasdecolombia.co), Version 11, 2025-12-05. Database of 565,394 persons.

### Line 1: +JUAN DE CAAMAÑO → Antioquia (THE COLOMBIA CONNECTION)

**Generation 1:** **+JUAN DE CAAMAÑO** — born Villa de Muros, La Coruña, Santiago, España. Married **+María Alfonso Martínez** (also from Muros).

**Generation 2:** **+Luis de Caamaño Martínez** — born Villa de Muros, 11 September 1647. Married 30 November 1682 with **+María Oáñez de Patiño** (daughter of +JUAN PATIÑO DE GUEMES, from Muros).

**Generation 3:** **+Juan Matías Martínez Perrúa y Caamaño** — born 21 February 1681, Villa de Muros. Married 3 September 1713 with **+Antonia de Relova Perrúa y Amúzar** (daughter of +LORENZO ANTONIO DE PERRÚA and +María Alonso de Amosar, from Muros).

**Generation 4:** **Bernardo Martínez Y Perrúa** — born Villa De Muros, Galicia, 19 August 1720. Died Santa Fe De Bogotá, 17 February 1788. **Arrived to Santa Fe de Antioquia in 1744.** Had 8 children.

**Key facts:**

- Origin: **Villa de Muros, La Coruña** — 25 km from Noia, same coast
- The surname changed from Caamaño → Martínez Perrúa y Caamaño → Martínez y Perrúa over generations (common pattern)
- Bernardo arrived in Colombia in 1744, settling in Antioquia
- The Patiño connection (Generation 2) matches the PARES Inquisition record for Felipe Caamaño de Ocampo + María Patiño (1699)

### Line 2: Clara De Caamaño De Mendoza (Noble Line)

**Clara De Caamaño De Mendoza** — born **Rubianes, Pontevedra, Galicia, 1502**. Married **Marqués De La Sierra De Outes Pedro Álvarez Mariño De Lobeira Y Valladares** (born Santiago De Compostela, 1493, died 1583).

Their son: **Cristobal Mariño De Lobeira Y Mendoza** — born Santiago De Compostela, 1517. IV Señor de la Sierra. Married Teresa Montenegro Pazos Y Sotomayor.

**Key facts:**

- "Caamaño De Mendoza" matches PARES records for Mendoza Caamaño noble line
- Rubianes is near Villagarcía de Arousa — same zone
- "Sierra De Outes" is a lordship near Noia — confirms the Noia/Ría de Arousa origin
- Date (1502) = one of the earliest documented Caamaño individuals

### Line 3: David Pontón Caamaño

David Pontón Caamaño married Julia de la Hortúa López. Son: Elvira Pontón de la Hortúa married Félix Alberto Malo Baños (15 May 1916).

### Line 4: Juana María Caamaño Y Vega (Ú Ortiz)

Married Francisco Prieto Pasaron. **5 children born in Santa Fe De Bogotá, 1689-1705:**

- Francisco Prieto Ortiz (b. 2 Oct 1689, Bogotá)
- Juan Dionisio Prieto Caamaño (b. 16 Oct 1690, Bogotá)
- Alejandro Prieto Caamaño (b. 14 Mar 1693, Bogotá)
- María Josefa Prieto Caamaño (b. 16 Apr 1695, Bogotá)
- María Teresa Prieto Pasarón Caamaño (b. 10 Oct 1705, Bogotá) — married Miguel de Azula y Murcia (from Vizcaya, Spain, 1696)

**Key fact:** Caamaños were in Bogotá by the 1680s-1690s. This is contemporary with Salvador Varela Caamaño's CONTRATACIÓN license (1687/1690).
