## The Gap — Connecting to Our Family

### What We Know

- **José Tomás Caamaño** (p001) born in "Caamaño, A Coruña, España" ~1840-1860
- Married "Desconocida Soto" (also from Caamaño, A Coruña)
- Emigrated to Colombia
- Brothers emigrated to DR (José María), Ecuador (Salvador), Argentina (unknown name)

### Possible Connections

1. **José Tomás = José Caamaño Incógnito?** — son of José Caamaño González with missing surname. If he emigrated young, local records would be incomplete.
2. **José Tomás = brother of José Caamaño González?** — both born ~1830s-1840s, sons of Pablo Caamaño Villa
3. **José Tomás = son of Felipe Caamaño?** — Felipe had sons in Palmeira ~1851, right era
4. **José Tomás from the parish of Santa María de Caamaño (Porto do Son)** — not Palmeira/Ribeira. Adjacent but different parish. The FamilySearch municipal records may not cover Porto do Son.

### Critical Note

The FamilySearch "La Coruña Municipal Records" collection only covers **Betanzos, Ferrol, and parts of Ribeira**. It does NOT cover Porto do Son directly. José Tomás's birth record would be in the **church parish records** of Santa María de Caamaño (under Diocese of Santiago de Compostela), which are in a DIFFERENT collection or not yet digitized.
